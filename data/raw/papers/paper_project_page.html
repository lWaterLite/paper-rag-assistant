Retrieval-Augmented Generation Evaluation Notes

Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems should be evaluated as a pipeline, not only as a language model response.

Retrieval Quality

HitRate@k, Recall@k and MRR help identify whether the retriever brings the correct evidence into the candidate set.

Generation Quality

Faithfulness and citation correctness are important because users need to verify whether the answer is grounded in the provided context.
